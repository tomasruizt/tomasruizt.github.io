**New Kernel Release: FMMS keeps LLM sampling on-chip. It beats FlashInfer and PyTorch compiled sampling**

The LM head writes the full logits tensor to GMEM. The sampler immediately reads them back. This round-trip is wasteful in the memory-bound decoding regime. FMMS replaces both with one Triton kernel that does matmul + sampling in SRAM.

Key results:

Kernel microbenchmarks (batch 1-64, GPUs: H100 to B300):
- Up to 1.52x faster than torch compiled baselines
- Up to 1.25x faster than FlashInfer sampling kernels

End-to-end (!) benchmarks on vLLM (GPU: B200):
- 19% lower TPOT on Qwen3-1.7B
- 3-7% on Qwen3-8B
- 1-5% on Qwen3-32B and gpt-oss-120b

The key insight: the Gumbel-max trick turns sampling into a per-tile argmax, so you never need to materialize the full logit matrix. Each tile computes its local winner, a tiny cross-tile reduction picks the global winner. That's it. No softmax, no multinomial, no GMEM traffic.
