**New post: FMMS: A Fused Matmul-Sample Kernel for LLM Inference**

During LLM decoding, the LM head computes logits and writes them to global memory, only for the sampler to read them back immediately. FMMS fuses these two steps into a single Triton kernel using the Gumbel-max trick, keeping logits in SRAM and never materializing the full logits tensor.

In this post I cover:
* The Gumbel-max reparameterization and why argmax decomposes over tiles, enabling tile-by-tile fusion of matmul and sampling.
* Kernel microbenchmarks on B300, B200, H200, and H100: up to 1.52x faster than torch compiled baselines and up to 1.25x faster than FlashInfer in the memory-bound regime (batch sizes 1-64).
* End-to-end vLLM integration: TPOT reductions of 11-19% on Qwen3-1.7B and up to 29% on Qwen3-8B on B200.
* Correctness verification via chi-squared tests and GSM8K accuracy (no statistically significant difference, p=0.776).
