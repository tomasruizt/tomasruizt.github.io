**New post: FMMS: A Fused Matmul-Sample Kernel for LLM Inference**

During LLM decoding, the LM head computes logits and writes them to global memory, only for the sampler to read them back immediately. FMMS fuses these two steps into a single Triton kernel using the Gumbel-max trick, keeping logits in SRAM and preventing round-trips to GMEM.

In this post I cover:
* Kernel microbenchmarks: up to 1.52x faster than torch compiled baselines and up to 1.25x faster than FlashInfer in the memory-bound regime (batch sizes 1-64, 4 GPUs from H100 to B300).
* End-to-end vLLM integration on B200: up to 19% lower TPOT on Qwen3-1.7B, 3-7% on Qwen3-8B, and 1-5% on larger models (Qwen3-32B, gpt-oss-120b).
* The Gumbel-max reparameterization that enables tile-by-tile fusion of matmul and sampling.
